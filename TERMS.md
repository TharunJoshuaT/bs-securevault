## 2. Zero-Knowledge Architecture & Absolute User Responsibility

### ⚠️ IMPORTANT SECURITY NOTICE: NO PASSWORD RECOVERY
BS SecureVault is engineered as a strictly **Zero-Knowledge, Client-Side Cryptographic System**:
* **No Master Keys:** BS OS SE does not generate, store, transmit, or possess master decryption keys or backdoors.
* **No Server Storage:** All key derivation (Argon2id) and authenticated encryption (AES-256-GCM) execute entirely within local computer memory or client browser space.
* **Unrecoverable Passphrases:** If you lose, forget, or misplace your Master Passphrase, **your encrypted data is mathematically unrecoverable**. BS OS SE support personnel cannot restore, reset, or bypass your encryption under any circumstances.
